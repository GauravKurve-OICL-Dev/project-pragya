# PROJECT PRAGYA
## Design Principles

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Project:** Enterprise Knowledge & Decision Support Platform  
**Organization:** The Oriental Insurance Company Ltd.

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the fundamental design principles that govern the architecture, functionality, governance, and future evolution of PROJECT PRAGYA.

These principles ensure that every functional, technical, and governance decision remains aligned with the strategic vision of the organization.

---

# 2. Vision Statement

PROJECT PRAGYA is envisioned as the Digital Institutional Memory of The Oriental Insurance Company Ltd.

Its purpose is to preserve organizational knowledge, enable intelligent knowledge discovery, support informed decision-making, and facilitate continuous organizational learning through a governed enterprise knowledge platform.

---

# 3. Core Design Principles

## Principle 1
### Business First – Technology Second

PRAGYA is designed to solve business problems rather than demonstrate technology.

Technology decisions shall always support business objectives and organizational governance.

---

## Principle 2
### Human Authority • AI Assistance

Artificial Intelligence shall function exclusively as an assistant.

AI may:

- Retrieve information
- Summarize knowledge
- Cross-reference documents
- Recommend relevant sources
- Draft documents based on approved organizational knowledge

AI shall never:

- Create company policies
- Modify organizational knowledge
- Override competent authority
- Issue business decisions autonomously

Final authority shall always remain with the designated organizational authority.

---

## Principle 3
### Knowledge is an Organizational Asset

Knowledge belongs to the organization rather than individual employees.

PRAGYA shall preserve institutional knowledge so that expertise remains available regardless of transfers, retirements, or organizational restructuring.

---

## Principle 4
### Governance Before Automation

Every item of organizational knowledge shall have an identified owner.

No interpretation, clarification, FAQ, or procedural guidance shall become part of the enterprise knowledge base without approval by the designated authority.

---

## Principle 5
### Authoritative Knowledge Takes Precedence

When multiple knowledge sources exist, PRAGYA shall always prioritize authoritative organizational sources.

The knowledge hierarchy shall be:

1. Company Circulars
2. Company Manuals
3. Policy Wordings & Product Documents
4. Regulatory Documents
5. Approved Organizational Knowledge
6. Educational Reference Material

---

## Principle 6
### AI Responses Shall Be Explainable

Every AI-generated response shall identify:

- Source documents
- Applicable circulars
- Confidence level
- Related references

Employees shall always be able to verify the origin of an answer.

---

## Principle 7
### Continuous Organizational Learning

Questions without authoritative answers shall become candidates for organizational learning.

Approved clarifications shall be incorporated into the enterprise knowledge base for future use.

---

## Principle 8
### Knowledge Should Be Discoverable

Employees shall be able to locate organizational knowledge through:

- Natural language search
- Structured browsing
- Department-wise navigation
- Metadata filters
- Cross-linked related documents

---

## Principle 9
### Assist the Work, Not Just the Search

PRAGYA shall support employees beyond information retrieval by providing contextual assistance based on approved knowledge.

Examples include:

- SOP guidance
- Checklists
- Draft office notes
- Draft letters
- Related forms
- Applicable procedures

Such assistance shall always remain based upon approved organizational knowledge.

---

## Principle 10
### Enterprise by Design

PRAGYA shall be designed as an enterprise platform capable of serving all departments, offices, and organizational levels of The Oriental Insurance Company Ltd.

The architecture shall support future integration with enterprise systems while maintaining modularity and governance.

---

Principle 11
Preserve Context, Not Just Content

Organizational knowledge is more than documents. It includes the relationships, historical decisions, interpretations, precedents, and practical application of those documents.
PRAGYA shall preserve both the content of organizational knowledge and the context in which that knowledge is applied, enabling future employees to benefit from the accumulated institutional wisdom of the organization.

Principle 12
Security, Privacy & Compliance by Design

Since PRAGYA will eventually contain confidential organizational knowledge, it should be designed from day one with security and governance in mind.
For example:
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Department-wise permissions
Audit logging
Document version history
Access logging
Encryption
Compliance with organizational policies
This reinforces that PRAGYA is an enterprise platform, not a consumer AI application.

# 4. Design Philosophy

PROJECT PRAGYA is not an AI chatbot.

It is an Enterprise Knowledge & Decision Support Platform that combines authoritative organizational knowledge, governed organizational learning, and AI-assisted knowledge discovery to improve the quality, consistency, and efficiency of organizational decision support.

---

# 5. Future Evolution

The initial implementation focuses on enterprise knowledge management.

Future versions may integrate with operational enterprise systems such as Policy Administration, Claims Management, HRMS, ERP, and Workflow platforms, subject to organizational approval and implementation priorities.
