# 06. AI Design Principles

## Objective
AI within PROJECT PRAGYA should enhance decision-making without compromising trust, governance, or human oversight.

## Design Principles
### 1. Grounded Responses
AI responses must be based on approved organizational knowledge and traceable sources.

### 2. Human-in-the-Loop
Critical decisions should remain under human review, especially where compliance or risk is involved.

### 3. Transparency
The system should explain why an answer was produced and what sources informed it.

### 4. Safety and Privacy
Sensitive information must be protected, and AI interactions should follow enterprise security policies.

### 5. Accuracy Over Ambition
The platform should prioritize reliability and useful constraints over overly broad or speculative outputs.

### 6. Continuous Improvement
Feedback, usage patterns, and review outcomes should improve prompts, retrieval logic, and knowledge quality over time.

## Recommended AI Use Cases
- Semantic document search
- Summarization of lengthy policies and procedures
- Contextual retrieval for decision support
- Knowledge recommendations and onboarding assistance
- Drafting support for approved response templates

## Expected Outcome
AI will act as an intelligent assistant that supports users while staying aligned with enterprise standards and governance controls.
