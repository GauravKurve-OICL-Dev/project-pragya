# 04. Knowledge Sources and Hierarchy

## Knowledge Sources
PROJECT PRAGYA will integrate knowledge from multiple internal and external sources, including:
- Policy manuals and procedural documents
- Claims and underwriting case files
- Regulatory circulars and compliance notices
- Product brochures and service documents
- Training material and presentations
- Standard operating procedures
- FAQs and approved response templates
- Structured enterprise systems and databases

## Source Classification
### Primary Sources
Authoritative documents that define policy, process, and approval guidelines.

### Secondary Sources
Reference material that supports interpretation and execution.

### Tertiary Sources
Contextual or historical knowledge, such as prior decisions and examples.

## Knowledge Hierarchy
### Level 1 – Strategic Knowledge
Corporate strategy, governance principles, and regulatory direction.

### Level 2 – Operational Knowledge
Standard processes, workflows, and procedural documentation.

### Level 3 – Case Knowledge
Examples, precedents, and past decisions that support practical application.

### Level 4 – User Context
Role-based insights, frequently asked questions, and situational guidance.

## Source Prioritization
When multiple sources provide overlapping information, the system should prefer:
1. Approved policy documents
2. Current operational procedures
3. Reviewed precedent cases
4. User-generated notes or drafts with proper status

## Expected Outcome
The hierarchy ensures that AI and users receive the most relevant, accurate, and trusted knowledge depending on the context.
