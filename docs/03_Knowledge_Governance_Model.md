# 03. Knowledge Governance Model

## Purpose
The Knowledge Governance Model defines how knowledge is curated, approved, maintained, and used responsibly in PROJECT PRAGYA.

## Governance Objectives
- Ensure accuracy and consistency of enterprise knowledge
- Maintain accountability for content ownership
- Protect confidential and regulated information
- Create transparent review and approval workflows
- Support auditability and compliance reporting

## Governance Principles
1. Content ownership is explicit and assigned.
2. High-impact knowledge requires review before publication.
3. Sensitive knowledge is restricted by role and classification.
4. Version history is preserved for traceability.
5. AI use must respect approved sources and governance policies.

## Governance Lifecycle
### Create
Knowledge is authored or imported by business users or system integrations.

### Review
Subject matter experts and knowledge stewards validate the content for relevance and correctness.

### Approve
Authorized approvers confirm readiness for publication.

### Publish
Approved content becomes available to the intended audience.

### Maintain
Content is periodically reviewed, updated, retired, or archived.

## Governance Roles
- Knowledge Owner: accountable for the content domain
- Knowledge Steward: manages content quality and lifecycle
- Reviewer: validates accuracy and business relevance
- Approver: authorizes publication
- Administrator: manages roles, permissions, and platform settings

## Expected Outcome
The governance model will provide trust, consistency, and control while enabling collaboration and innovation.
